# Situational Leadership — 情景领导力

## Theory Origin

Developed by **Paul Hersey** and **Kenneth Blanchard** (1975, Center for Leadership Studies). Used at Microsoft, GE, IBM, GM. Core idea: **no single best leadership style — the best style depends on the follower's readiness level.**

---

## Two Core Dimensions

| Dimension | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| **工作成熟度** (Task Maturity) | Knowledge, skills, experience — ability to do the job |
| **心理成熟度** (Psychological Maturity) | Willingness, motivation, confidence — readiness to do it |

> 成熟度 = 能力 × 意愿的组合，不是单纯的"能不能干"

---

## Four Employee Development Stages (R1–R4)

| Stage | Ability | Willingness | Characteristic |
|-------|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **R1** | Low | **High** | Enthusiastic beginner — motivated but lacks skills |
| **R2** | Low→Medium | Medium | Can do some tasks, still needs guidance |
| **R3** | Medium→High | Fluctuating | Capable but confidence wavers |
| **R4** | High | **High** | Fully capable, willing, and confident |

> ⚠️ **Common error**: R1 is NOT "unable + unwilling" — that description is wrong. R1 = **unable + willing** (enthusiastic beginner).

### R→S Direction
```
R1 → R2 → R3 → R4
↑__________________↑
   S1   S2   S3   S4
(Leader-directed)    (Follower-directed)
```

---

## Four Leadership Styles (S1–S4)

| Style | Task Behavior | Relationship Behavior | Alias | Description |
|-------|--------------|----------------------|-------|-------------|
| **S1 指令型** | High | Low | 告知式 | Direct orders, set standards, minimal explanation |
| **S2 教练型** | High | High | 推销式 | Explain why, provide support, two-way communication |
| **S3 支持型** | Low | High | 参与式 | Listen, encourage participation, shared decision-making |
| **S4 授权型** | Low | Low | 放手式 | Full trust, let them own it, available when needed |

---

## R-S Matching Matrix

| Employee Readiness | Leadership Style | Key Actions |
|-------------------|-----------------|-------------|
| R1 (low ability + high willingness) | **S1 Directive** | Hand-hold teaching, set goals, give steps |
| R2 (low ability + medium willingness) | **S2 Coaching** | Teach + encourage, explain reasoning, two-way |
| R3 (high ability + fluctuating confidence) | **S3 Supportive** | Recognize more, give space, involve in decisions |
| R4 (high ability + high confidence) | **S4 Delegating** | Full放手, let them lead |

---

## Key Adjustment Principles

**For R1/R2 (low readiness):**
1. First: **reduce directive behavior** as skills improve
2. Then: **increase relationship behavior** as reward for good performance
> Order matters: reduce task behavior FIRST, then increase relationship behavior

**For R3/R4 (high readiness):**
- Watch for confidence changes, adjust授权程度 accordingly

---

## Limitations (Robbins Critique)

1. **内在模糊性**：判断下属准备度主观性强
2. **效度存疑**：直觉有亲和力，效果不一定可靠
3. **衡量难度**：成熟度缺乏客观测量标准

> 实用建议：大量案例、角色演练、实战模拟来培养判断力

## Growth vs Fixed Mindset

| | Growth 成长型 | Fixed 固定型 |
|---|---|---|
| Core belief | Ability can be developed | Ability is innate |
| To challenge | Welcome as growth opportunity | Avoid as threat |
| To failure | Learn from it | Avoid or deny |
| Best for | Innovation, breakthrough | Stable process, efficient execution |

> **Key insight**: Fixed mindset is an environment problem, not an ability problem.

---

## Non-Authority Leadership (横向影响力)

When you have no direct reporting authority:

| Strategy | When to Use |
|----------|-------------|
| **建立信任** | Build respect through expertise and consistency |
| **展示价值** | Show the benefit of cooperation |
| **找到共同目标** | Emphasize shared interests |
| **影响决策层** | Go up the chain when lateral fails |
| **先付出** | Offer help first, build reciprocity |

## Application Scenarios

- Direct reports management (有职权)
- Cross-functional collaboration (无职权)
- Dealing with different readiness levels (R1–R4)
- Dealing with different mindsets (Growth vs Fixed)
- Children's leadership development (传递给下一代)
