# MQL Lead Validation Methodology

## Critical Rule
**NEVER fabricate contact names or information.** When information is unknown, mark it as "待核实" (to be verified) rather than inventing details.

## Validation Hierarchy (in priority order)

### 1. Official Dealer/Partner Pages
- Hach China: https://www.hach.com.cn/dealer
- Search by company name to find authorized dealers

### 2. Company Official Website
- Always check the company's own "联系我们" (Contact Us) page
- Extract actual names, phones, emails from the page

### 3. Official Communications
- Official announcements, procurement platforms
- Business registration databases

### 4. Third-Party Verification
- Industry events, conference attendee lists
- LinkedIn, professional networks

## Don't Do This
- Inventing contact names because you cannot find them
- Recording unverified information as fact
- Assuming generic titles without verification

## Do This Instead
- Mark unknown contacts as "待核实"
- Search official sources first (dealer page, company website)
- Record the source URL for every piece of information

## Example: Hach Shandong Channel

**Wrong approach** (what I did)
- Invented "李玉池" — fabrication, had to correct later

**Correct approach**
1. Search Hach dealer page, found 青岛首创科技 (authorized dealer)
2. Visit company website contact page, extracted actual contacts
3. Record with source URL

## Verification Checklist
- Company name verified from official dealer page
- Contact name extracted from company website
- Phone/email from official source
- Source URL recorded
