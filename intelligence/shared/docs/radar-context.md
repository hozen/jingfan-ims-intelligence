# Shared iMS GTM radar context

This is the repository-safe subset of context shared by the industrial and municipal radars. Market-specific search instructions are in each market's prompt and specifications.

## Common purpose

Find public, evidence-backed changes that could create a relevant Hach iMS opportunity, early enough for sales to influence the project. Distinguish demand for water/process instrumentation from demand for iMS software and services; one does not prove the other.

## Evidence and qualification

- Label public facts, reasoned inferences, and unknowns separately. Link claims to public source URLs and state what still needs verification.
- Do not invent contacts, roles, budgets, software deployments, project stages, or customer intent.
- Treat an opportunity hypothesis as qualified only when the market-specific gate is met. Keep rejected or weak signals in the proper exclusion/background section rather than promoting them to opportunities.
- Preserve source identity, dates, continuity, and uncertainty when carrying a signal between reports or stages.
- Apply the shared [Group Software Evidence Check](Group_Software_Evidence_Check_v1.0.md) to both markets.

## Market separation

- **Industrial:** focus on relevant process industries and early compliance, investment, capacity, facility, and project signals. Industrial facilities such as fabs belong to this radar.
- **Municipal:** focus on water/wastewater owners, utility and city investment, policy/funding, project preparation, and the associated design/EPC/integrator ecosystem.
- Shared iMS context does not make the two markets interchangeable. Keep their discovery engines, qualification gates, report JSON, IDs, and latest-feed interfaces distinct.

## Deliverables

Use Markdown and JSON. PDF is not a required radar deliverable. Production outputs stay in their established market directories; historical exports under `archive/` are not inputs to current lead pages or latest feeds.
